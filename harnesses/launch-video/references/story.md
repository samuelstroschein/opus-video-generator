# Story: beats, hooks, length

The story is what founders can judge fastest, and it is the part that is cheapest to fix, so get it right before polishing visuals.

## One claim, one proof

A launch video is an argument, not a tour. Write two sentences before planning beats: the **claim** (this product does X for you) and the **proof** (here is X happening, visible on screen). If the claim needs two sentences it is two videos; if the proof can't be shown, the brief isn't ready. Cut anything neither one needs. The test: a viewer who has never seen the product can say what changed and why it is good.

## Structures that work for a launch video

- **Problem → Product → Payoff** (default). The pain in one image, the product resolving it, the result and a call to action.
- **Before / After.** The same scene twice: chaos, then calm. Strong for tools that remove work.
- **One moment.** A single request typed in, then the product doing it, end to end. Strong for AI products.
- **Feature tour.** Only when the launch is several features; one beat per feature, shortest first.

## Beats

- Count by length: 15s → 3–4 beats, 30s → 5–6, 45s → 6–7. More beats means less time to read.
- **Hook in the first 3 seconds**: a surprising number, a sharp contrast, or the product already doing the impossible thing. Never open on a logo.
- Each beat has one job (hook, problem, turn, proof, payoff, call to action) and one idea.
- The call to action is the last beat: the product name and URL, held long enough to read.
- On-screen text: 2–7 words per beat, in the product's voice, readable in the beat's time (about 3 words per second plus the reveal).
- Use the real product name and real features. Never claim something the site does not.

## If the user supplies a reference video

The reference gives pacing, type and transitions to copy. Break it into beats: what happens in each, how each scene turns into the next, the camera moves, the colors and fonts. Then write the same structure for this product. (Automatic frame extraction is not available in this app yet: if the user describes the reference or pastes frames, work from that.)
